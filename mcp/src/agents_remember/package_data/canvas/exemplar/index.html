Retry jitter cap in a job queue

exemplar · made-up change

Capping exponential backoff at 80% of the queue lease and sampling full jitter keeps retrying workers inside their lease window and breaks lockstep retry storms.

In this illustrative queue, each worker holds a 30 s lease while processing a job. Previously, src/queue/retry.ts:12 doubled the base delay without a ceiling, so a job failing five times waited 64 s—more than twice its own lease illustrative. The queue expired the lease mid-wait and handed the same job to a second worker, causing duplicate runs. The change adds a lease-derived ceiling of 0.8 * leaseMs and samples uniformly across [0, capped] model rule.
