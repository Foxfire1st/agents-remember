# Draft Author Result Notes

This draft case captures the framework shape for the Agents Remember drift workflow benchmark.

The initial local observations that motivated this benchmark family were:

- On very small repositories, onboarding can cost more time and tokens than it saves.
- On larger repositories, mature onboarding can reduce exploration cost and prevent architectural misclassification.
- The most reproducible tasks are explanation and debugging tasks with stable completion criteria.
