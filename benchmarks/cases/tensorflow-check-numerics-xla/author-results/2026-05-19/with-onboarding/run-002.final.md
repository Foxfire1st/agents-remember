Most likely cause: under `jit_compile=True`, XLA does not lower `CheckNumerics` to a runtime numerical check. It allows the op into the compiled cluster, then legalizes it away as an identity/pass-through because HLO lacks native support for that side-effecting assertion. That exactly explains why eager and non-JIT graph execution raise, while XLA JIT silently returns the NaN-containing tensor.
