# Visual-explainer authoring guidance

Use the [shared STE-inspired writing profile](shared-writing-profile.md) for walkthrough prose,
labels and narrated obligations. This is an application of that profile, not a second style guide.

Bind the explanation to the exact candidate and cite the invariant revision or family guarantee
it explains. Reuse existing term records and product terms. Keep record identifiers, quoted source
text and admitted effect labels exact. Show the promise, its actor and trigger, and the applicable
conditions and exclusions together; a diagram must not hide a condition in an unlinked legend.

Separate a promise from its realization and proof. An arrow may explain responsibility or flow;
it does not establish a test result. Link the actual evidence and name its scope and limitations.
Distinguish missing, unexamined, unavailable and measured absence instead of rendering all four as
an empty success state. A family walkthrough explains its authored joint guarantee, not an
inferred guarantee based on grouping its members visually.

When paraphrasing a recorded obligation, preserve the exact source text beside or behind an
explicit link. Submit the proposed paraphrase and its conforming, violating and boundary cases to
the profile's independent meaning review. A shorter label is not proof that the obligation stayed
the same. Keep an ambiguous explanation unaccepted until it is corrected or the actual change is
classified through the normal writer/history/revision route. Never edit frozen records or closed
history to match the diagram, and never present an author's comparison as independent acceptance.

An optional walkthrough supplements the required report and its exact evidence. It cannot replace
schema, admission or invariant checks, an independent review, or a requirement acceptance record.
