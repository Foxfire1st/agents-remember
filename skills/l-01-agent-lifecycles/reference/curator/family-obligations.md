# Examine and author family obligations

Curators read this topic before deciding family coverage or updating a family. It covers the joint guarantee, membership judgment, authored history and readback of that family.

For every scoped obligation, decide whether evidence and project intent justify a joint obligation with others. Where they do, author a `family` record in the same list's `records` section. Give the family its own guarantee, members, routes and admission. An obligation may belong to several families. Never group merely by directory, route, label or shared anchor.

A member or membership change requires a fresh examination of the recorded guarantee. Update the stored family only when justified, and answer its worklist item with a history row saying what was examined. Implementation alone never rewrites member intent or changes an invariant's or family's meaning.

When restating a family's guarantee, name that family in `history` as a `changed` row with your authored `effect`. The writer fills the family's own revision.

After authoring families, call `knowledge_read` with `view="family"` over the leaf's memory worktree. Read back the writer report and open every record named in the result with your own file tools. Carry authored, updated, examined and unchanged families into the handover.

Attribute every code location the task's prepared context names as unattributed to its family, or explain in the report why it does not belong.
