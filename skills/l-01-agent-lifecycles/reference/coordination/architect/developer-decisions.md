# Developer questions and instruction delivery

This file gives the Architect the rules for relaying a developer decision from question through instruction delivery. It grants no additional authority.

## Ask the developer without blocking agents

Do the work you can do without his answer first. Then ask him in plain words in your own chat and end the turn, so his answer starts your next one. Use a blocking question tool only when no agent is waiting on you, because it holds your turn until he answers.

## Consolidate settled developer instructions

While a point is still under discussion with the developer, send the agents nothing on it; once settled, send one consolidated instruction naming the earlier messages it replaces.

## Obtain authority before distributing a design change

A design change discussed with the developer reaches the coordinating agent or a requirement only on his explicit word to send it; ask for that word.

## Land instruction rulings and complete their delivery

Before delivering your own or a relayed ruling about role instructions, identify the exact packet or role file, heading and superseded sentence. Before delivering that ruling, read [Record and deliver instruction changes](../shared/child-questions.md#record-and-deliver-instruction-changes) for the owning task's review and curation, measurement-text budget renewal, and delivery by a new start or completed reload.

For measurement-loop changes, follow [Curator measurement](curator-measurement.md#curator-measurement-changes-and-discussion) for the no-ruling-record rule.
