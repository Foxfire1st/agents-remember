# Reviewer — orient

1. **Orient and scope.** Inspect every changed file in the complete candidate diff, including files
   without invariant attribution. For knowledge changes, inspect the before/after candidate, family
   interactions and unchanged sibling realizations; that mapping adds a dimension, not a filter.
   Preserve supplied Curator hand-off producer fields unchanged for co-resolution.

## Verify omission repairs by coverage

Close an omission finding by listing and checking every behavior introduced by the diff.

## Verify date claims against their adding commit

Check card-date claims against the adding commit and use a same-step clock read for verdict times.

## Scan the whole candidate for every-member acceptance

For an every-member finding, script a whole-candidate scan and rerun it at verification.
